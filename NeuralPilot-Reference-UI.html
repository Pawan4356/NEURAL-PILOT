    From a CSV to a validated model, in a loop you can read.

    1. Understand

      Finds the target column and what you care about.

    2. Plan

      Picks one pipeline from trusted scikit-learn, XGBoost and CatBoost parts.

    3. Verify and execute

      Checks the plan, then trains, tunes and validates. The LLM never writes code.

    4. Reflect and decide

      Accepts the result, refines it, replans or stops.

    Agent reasonsEngine does the work

    Reflection feeds back into planning until the result is accepted or the iteration limit is reached.
